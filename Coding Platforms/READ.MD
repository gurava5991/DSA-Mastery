
## 🚀 Top Coding Platforms for SDE Prep

### 🔹 **1. LeetCode**

* 📌 Best for: **Interview-focused DSA problems**
* 🔥 Features:

    * Huge problem bank categorized by companies (FAANG, etc.)
    * Mock assessments & contests
    * Editorials + community solutions
* 🌍 [Visit LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/)

---

### 🔹 **2. Codeforces**

* 📌 Best for: **Competitive programming & contests**
* 🔥 Features:

    * Regular rated contests (Div 1/2/3)
    * Problems improve **speed + problem-solving IQ**
    * Editorials for every contest
* 🌍 [Visit Codeforces](https://codeforces.com/)

---

### 🔹 **3. CodeChef**

* 📌 Best for: **DSA learning + long contests**
* 🔥 Features:

    * Long challenges (10 days) + Cook-Offs + Lunchtime
    * Good platform to build **rating & consistency**
    * Rich discussion forum & tutorials
* 🌍 [Visit CodeChef](https://www.codechef.com/)

---

### 🔹 **4. HackerRank**

* 📌 Best for: **Beginners & company-specific prep**
* 🔥 Features:

    * Easy-to-start interface
    * Certifications in DSA, SQL, Python, etc.
    * Used in actual **company hiring tests**
* 🌍 [Visit HackerRank](https://www.hackerrank.com/)

---

### 🔹 **5. GeeksforGeeks (GFG)**

* 📌 Best for: **Learning + practicing DSA systematically**
* 🔥 Features:

    * Topic-wise problem sets
    * Detailed tutorials & articles
    * Company-wise coding questions
* 🌍 [Visit GFG](https://practice.geeksforgeeks.org/)

---

### 🔹 **6. AtCoder**

* 📌 Best for: **Mathematics-heavy competitive programming**
* 🔥 Features:

    * Beginner & regular contests
    * High-quality problems
    * Great for **developing strong fundamentals**
* 🌍 [Visit AtCoder](https://atcoder.jp/)

---

### 🔹 **7. InterviewBit**

* 📌 Best for: **Interview preparation roadmap**
* 🔥 Features:

    * Structured syllabus for coding interviews
    * Mock interviews & company questions
    * Great for **step-by-step prep**
* 🌍 [Visit InterviewBit](https://www.interviewbit.com/)

---

👉 If your **goal is interviews**, I’d suggest this **priority order**:
**LeetCode → GFG → InterviewBit → HackerRank**

👉 If your **goal is competitive programming (CP)**:
**Codeforces → AtCoder → CodeChef**
